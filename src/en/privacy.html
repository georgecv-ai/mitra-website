Privacy Notice

Effective: September 7, 2026

1. Who We Are

Mitra is an AI-powered tutoring service for middle and high school students, with expertise in supporting students with ADHD and other learning differences. Sessions are delivered through the Mitra app, the Mitra web portal, KakaoTalk, and Telegram.

The Mitra app is published and operated by The Coaching Record LLC (United States) on behalf of 미트라 AI (Mitra AI), the Korean entity that owns the service (business details below). Both operate under this notice.

2. Information About Children (COPPA)

Mitra is directed in part to children under 13. We collect personal information from children only with the prior verifiable consent of their parent or legal guardian, obtained through our enrollment process or when a parent creates their child's app or web access.

3. Information We Collect

From parents at enrollment: Name, email, phone number, student name, age, grade, school, learning profile (diagnoses, performance levels, attention patterns).

During tutoring sessions: All text messages sent to Mitra, homework photos voluntarily sent for tutoring help.

From the Mitra app: A device credential that keeps your child signed in, the device's push notification token (used only to deliver notifications), and any chat replies your child flags for our safety team.

Automatically generated: Session assessments, cross-session memory notes, parent engagement data.

We do NOT collect: Payment card numbers, government IDs, precise geolocation, biometric data, advertising identifiers, or contacts.

4. Third Parties

We share student information only as necessary to deliver the service:

  • Anthropic — AI model provider. Receives learning profile and conversation text to generate tutoring responses, processing them solely on our behalf. Does not use API data to train its models. Privacy policy
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — delivers app notifications to Android devices. Receives the device's push token and notification text. Privacy policy
  • Apple (Push Notification service) — delivers app notifications to iPhones and iPads. Receives the device's push token and notification text. Privacy policy
  • Twilio — SMS message delivery (parent OTP authentication). Receives phone numbers and message text. Privacy policy
  • KakaoTalk (Kakao Corp.) — Message delivery (primary channel for Korea). Receives Kakao user IDs and message text via the official channel webhook. Privacy policy
  • Telegram — Message delivery. Receives user IDs and message text. Standard chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Privacy policy
  • Railway — Cloud hosting (SOC 2 Type II certified). Privacy policy

We do not sell student data. We do not share student data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. AI Processing and Your Consent

Mitra's tutoring is powered by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. Your child's messages and learning profile are sent to Anthropic to generate tutoring responses. Anthropic processes this data solely to provide the service and does not use it to train its models. Anthropic retains chat data for up to 30 days; content flagged by its safety systems may be retained longer as required for safety and legal compliance.

We ask for your explicit consent to this processing before your child's first chat — at enrollment, or when you create your child's app or web access. You can withdraw consent at any time by resetting your child's access in the parent portal, deleting the account, or emailing support@mitratutor.com; withdrawal stops all further AI processing.

Every Mitra reply can be flagged from inside the chat. Flagged replies go to a human on our team for review, normally within 24 hours.

6. Data Retention

  • Student profile and learning data: retained while enrolled, deleted within 30 days of withdrawal or deletion request
  • Tutoring session content: retained while enrolled, deleted within 30 days of deletion request
  • Cross-session memory notes: auto-expire after 7 days unless referenced during an active session
  • Parent contact information: retained while account is active, deleted within 30 days of deletion request

7. Parental Rights

As the parent or guardian of an enrolled student, you have the right to:

  • Review your child's personal information
  • Correct any inaccurate information via the parent portal
  • Delete a student's data by unenrolling them on their student page, or delete your entire account (your parent account and all linked students) from Account → Delete account — in the app or at mitratutor.com. Full instructions, including deletion without portal access, are at mitratutor.com/account-deletion. Deletion completes within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by contacting us

8. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our infrastructure provider (Railway) is SOC 2 Type II certified with encryption at rest. Webhook endpoints validate signatures. All forms are CSRF-protected. Rate limiting is applied to public endpoints.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this privacy notice or your child's data: support@mitratutor.com
